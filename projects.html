Credit risk classification

Multiclass XGBoost model that sorts loan applicants into 4 risk tiers (P1 lowest risk to P4 highest). Uses statistical feature selection (Chi-square, ANOVA, VIF) to cut 88 features to 53, weighted sampling for class imbalance, saved model files and a Streamlit dashboard for single and batch predictions.
