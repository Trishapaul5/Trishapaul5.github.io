Main project
Haryana safety audit

Safecity collects reports of sexual harassment in public spaces and uses the data to push for safer communities. I worked on the Haryana safety audit data, which was updated daily, and turned it into dashboards and monthly reports for stakeholders.

View proof document ↗

What I did and what it achieved

Work

  • Analysed 22,000+ records from the raw audit form (55 columns) with Python and Excel.
  • Used Excel pivot tables and correlation analysis to compare transport mode, income, district, gender and helpline awareness against harassment reports.
  • Cleaned the data: removed records with no incident category and handled missing values and Hindi text.
  • Wrote Python cleaning workflows for 55-column datasets.
  • Built Power BI and Looker Studio dashboards covering 12+ regions.
  • Prepared monthly data reports and combined work from several team members into final reports.

Impact

  • Cut processing time by about 50% with reusable cleaning workflows.
  • The Haryana report was presented to police authorities and informed recommendations for community safety.
  • Dashboards made district-level trends and reasons for not reporting easy for stakeholders to see.
  • Delivered reports within tight 48-hour deadlines.
  • Received a strong recommendation from my programme manager for growth mindset and dedication.
